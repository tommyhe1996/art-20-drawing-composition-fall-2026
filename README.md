## Learning goals

By the end of the semester, I aim to:

- Create finished graphite and charcoal drawings using composition, implied direction, and positive/negative space.
- Translate three-dimensional form into two dimensions through proportion and shading.
- Explore abstraction, contour, gesture, portraiture, value, perspective, public-art design, hyperrealism, speed, looseness, and mood.
- Develop a personal drawing style and a stronger visual vocabulary for discussing art.

## Portfolio practice

I will photograph work in clear, even light; crop and straighten each image; use descriptive filenames; and record the date, medium, assignment, reflection, and instructor/peer feedback. The required Google Slides portfolio remains the official homework submission and grading location.

## Privacy and life-model policy

No photographs, video, screen captures, or other recordings of a live model will be stored in this repository. Only my own drawings may be documented, and only after the model session when permitted by the instructor.

This is a student-maintained learning portfolio. Canvas and instructor announcements are the authoritative sources for current requirements and deadlines.
